I am an undergraduate student in Computer Science at Hanoi University of Science and Technology (HUST) and a Research Assistant at the Center for AI Research (CAIR), VinUniversity, where I work on efficient Vision-Language-Action (VLA) models under the supervision of Duy M. H. Nguyen and Khoa D. Doan. My research focuses on developing scalable, efficient, and adaptive machine learning methods for continual learning, spanning both general machine learning and robot learning, and I am particularly interested in parameter-efficient fine-tuning and mixture-of-experts approaches that let models adapt to new tasks, domains, and environments with minimal computational and memory overhead.

I am also a Research Student at the Machine Learning Lab, SOICT, HUST, advised by Linh Ngo Van, where I study continual information extraction, using efficient fine-tuning to fight catastrophic forgetting and promote knowledge transfer in pretrained models. Alongside research, I was an AI Engineer Intern at PIXTA Vietnam, building an AI headshot generator with FLUX and DreamBooth.

Before university, I studied in the Special Mathematics class at Hoang Van Thu High School for Gifted Students, Hoa Binh, winning First Prize at the Provincial Mathematics Olympiad (2021, 2022) and qualifying for the Vietnamese Mathematics Olympiad twice.
